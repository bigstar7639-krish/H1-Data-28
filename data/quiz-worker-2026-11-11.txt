Q+A Katha - Motivation 🧠

Question: In "Magic: The Gathering", during the design for Planar Chaos, what color did the developers think of adding in as the sixth color?

Answer: Purple - Entertainment: Board Games. Itha kelvi Motivation pathi - Answer Purple. Motivation pathi kelvi patta oru ponnu, ava daily Motivation pathi yosichittu iruntha, oru naal ava oru puthu book padichu Motivation oda unmaiya purinjikita, athula irunthu ava life maariduchu, ava bayapadama Motivation ah face panna aarambicha, ava friends ellam ava maariya paathu aachariyapattanga, kadasiya ava Motivation la jeyichu santhosham ah iruntha, ithu thaan namakku kathai sollura paadam.
Q+A Katha - Peace 🧠

Question: Which portuguese island is soccer player Cristiano Ronaldo from?

Answer: Madeira - Sports. Itha kelvi Peace pathi - Answer Madeira. Oru naal oru chinna oor la oru paiyan irunthan, avan manasula Peace pathi neraya yosichittu irunthan, yaar kitta sollurathu nu theriyama thaniya irunthan, appo avanukku oru nalla friend kedaichan, antha friend avan kooda pesi avan manasa purinjikittan, kadasiya avan Peace la irunthu nalla purithaloda sirichuttu veetuku ponan, ithu thaan Peace oda azhagu, kashtam vanthalum nambikkaiya iruntha ellam sari aayidum nu avan purinjikittan.
Q+A Katha - Courage 🧠

Question: What does "hippopotamus" mean and in what langauge?

Answer: River Horse (Greek) - Animals. Itha kelvi Courage pathi - Answer River Horse (Greek). Oru kaalathula oru oor la Courage nu oru periya vishayam irunthuchu, yaarum atha purinjikama irunthanga, oru chinna paiyan thaan Courage oda arumaiya purinjikittan, avan Courage pathi ellaar kitta yum pesi puriya vachan, mella mella oor makkal ellam Courage ah purinjikittanga, antha oor ye Courage nala azhaga maariduchu, ithula irunthu namakku theriyura vishayam enna na, Courage ah purinjikita life ye azhagahum.
